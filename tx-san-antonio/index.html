Start with the issue in your home

Before contacting an HVAC contractor, think about which part of the system needs attention. Is the concern cooling, heating, airflow, ductwork, indoor air quality, or the thermostat? You don’t need to identify the technical cause. Simply describe what you experience, such as a room that stays uncomfortable or equipment that no longer starts as expected.

A short checklist before you hire

Use these points when you speak with an HVAC company:

  • Explain the problem and when it began.
  • Confirm the company can work on your equipment and the service you’re requesting.
  • Ask what the technician will check and how the findings will be explained.
  • Request the price in writing before authorizing work.
  • For a proposed system replacement, ask how the equipment will be sized for your home.

Mistakes to steer clear of

Don’t choose a contractor based only on a quick description of a symptom. A heating or cooling concern can have different causes, so the system needs to be checked before a repair plan is settled. Avoid agreeing to work when you’re unsure what it includes or what it will cost. Ask for a straightforward explanation and written pricing first.

It’s also easy to overlook maintenance until the equipment has a problem. If you’re planning ahead, ask what care makes sense for your system and how often it should be checked.
